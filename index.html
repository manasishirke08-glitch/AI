About CareerAI

Problem

Many students are unsure which career fits them or which skills to build.

Objective

Give personalised career, skill and job guidance in one place.

How the AI works

This is a rule-based recommendation engine. Each answer adds weights to 8 traits (coding, maths, data, security, design, people, business, infrastructure). Your trait vector is compared with every career using cosine similarity, which gives the match %.

Job match score

Skills 50% + career match 25% + education 15% + experience 10%.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript.

Limitations

Results are guidance, not guarantees. Jobs are samples. Accounts are a demo stored only in this browser. A real system needs Firebase or a similar backend for secure shared accounts and an admin panel.

Real vacancies: National Career Service, Internshala.

Help and FAQ

How do I create an account?

Open Login / Register, fill the form on the right and click Create account, or continue as a guest.

How do I get recommendations?

Login, open AI Assessment, answer all 10 questions and submit. Your top careers appear on the dashboard.

How do I save a career or job?

Open it and click Save. Saved items show on your dashboard.

Are the jobs real?

No. They are samples for the demo. Use the National Career Service for real vacancies.
